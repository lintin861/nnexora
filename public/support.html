Frequently Asked Questions

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Open Withdraw from your dashboard, enter an amount and your bank details, then submit. This project currently records a demo withdrawal and updates your balance and Activity; it does not send money to a bank.

What is the Nexora Key?

The key purchase is a demo flow. A submitted receipt is not proof of payment, and this demo does not automatically verify transfers or activate real withdrawal access.

What is the Tap & Earn feature?

Use the Earn page to see the available demo reward and claim it when the cooldown allows.

How do I upgrade my plan?

Plan upgrades are not available in this demo yet. Contact the support team after its Telegram link has been added.

Is my account secure?

Use a unique password and keep it private. You can update your password from Profile > Security Settings. Never share your password or one-time codes in a support chat.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawals are currently simulated by the project and are not sent to a bank, so there is no real transfer or settlement time.
